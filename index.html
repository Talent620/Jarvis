FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data secure and private?
Absolutely. Jarvis is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and never uses your data to train shared models. Your workspace is fully isolated.
How long does setup take?
Most teams are live in under ten minutes. Connect your tools, set your preferences, and Jarvis starts working immediately — no engineering required.
Which tools does Jarvis integrate with?
Email, calendar, docs, CRMs, and dozens of popular apps out of the box — with an open API for anything custom your team needs.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. Cancel or change your plan whenever you like, right from your dashboard.
Do you offer a free trial?
The Starter plan is free forever, and Pro comes with a 14-day trial — no credit card required to begin.
